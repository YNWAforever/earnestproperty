## Verification boundary and exclusions

This is source/identity/license verification. Exact remote resolution links were opened and the research connector reported image responses. It returned only text `ImageDisplayed` markers and URLs, not image content blocks. **No image pixels, native-browser appearance or social-crawler availability were verified.** Those limits also apply to the four selected sources; no pixel verification is implied by the coverage count. Current hero/share markup is checked separately in the preview. Generic category membership alone was insufficient for uncertain scenes.

All 40 gaps were searched using English/Chinese names, aliases, Commons categories/file pages and relevant street/development categories. No competitor image is fetched or copied; incidental listing-portal search results are excluded. Missing means no qualifying source found in this bounded pass, not proof that no photo exists.

Notable exclusions:

- **Peak Castle, Jade Grove and Crown By The Sea:** their Commons pages publish CC terms but also carry prose excluding named social platforms. No new image with that conflicting wording is selected. Peak Castle remains missing.
- **Dragon Inn Court:** `Yung lung.JPG` identifies the estate only in a small annotated part of a much wider scene. It is not promoted as a dedicated estate hero.
- **Bayview Terrace:** two CC BY-SA 4.0 candidates have an estate category but only a generic road description. Without pixels, estate prominence is unresolved; both remain conditional, not wired into the hero.
- **The Royale / OMA OMA / OMA by the Sea:** available matches were adverts, construction or scaffold-removal shots. They are not labelled completed estate exteriors.
- **Long Beach Gardens:** its exact-name file depicts a door/address number, not an estate exterior.
