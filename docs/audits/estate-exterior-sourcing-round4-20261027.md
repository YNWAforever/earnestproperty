Details below. |

The open DAP-L0 route is materially different from the restricted sample-photo page. The [public CKAN record](https://data.gov.hk/en-data/api/3/action/package_show?id=hk-landsd-openmap-development-hkms-digital-aerial-photo) resolved the exact dataset and its [CSDI resource](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/geoportal/?datasetId=64fc4fa4-3024-58a1-aac0-715b43021f82). CSDI retrieval failed; HKMS exposed only its map/search shell, without an identifiable photo for any missing estate. The [HKMS FAQ](https://www.hkmapservice.gov.hk/OneStopSystem/faqDownloadOpenDigitalMaps) confirms DAP-L0 is open data and describes reCaptcha on downloads. No challenge bypass or download was attempted.

Null license fields / `isopen: false` in the older CKAN metadata are **not** a finding that all DAP-L0 imagery is non-open. Conversely, the open-data terms do not supply a verified estate subject, capture year or usable photo URL by themselves, nor override every linked product's terms. An unpopulated map UI's zero counters are not zero-photo evidence. No map, generic regional aerial, rendering or unverified crop is substituted as an estate exterior.

The eastern subquery returned the same two. Neither is selected. Wider Hong Kong/regional responses were character-limited discovery, not exhaustive pagination; no absence claim is based on those partial responses.
- **KartaView:** read the documented public proximity-query implementation. The primary terms pages could not be retrieved, and the public photo query failed with `CRAWL_SSL_ERROR`. No image record or primary grant was verified. TLS validation was not disabled, and no proxy/access workaround was used. This is an access limitation, not evidence of zero coverage.

No protected street-view screenshots were captured. Metadata and license links do not establish what a photograph prominently depicts.
